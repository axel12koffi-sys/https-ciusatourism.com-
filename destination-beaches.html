Beaches & coastline

Five hundred kilometres of Atlantic, lagoons behind the dunes and a coast road that makes most of it reachable in a day.

Beaches & coastline

From lagoon to ocean,
east to west.

The coast runs from the Ghanaian border to Liberia. To the east, the lagoons and coconut groves of Assinie and Grand-Bassam, an hour or two from Abidjan. To the west it turns wilder, down to Sassandra and San Pedro. Each stretch has its own character, and you can chain them or keep just one.

6 places

Where we take
our travellers

45 min from Abidjan

Grand-Bassam

The country's first colonial capital. Its Quartier France has been on the UNESCO World Heritage list since 2012 — crumbling red-brick facades, the national costume museum, and the beach at the end of every street. It's where Abidjan goes on Sunday, and the easiest place to understand the country's history without leaving the coast.

  • UNESCO-listed Quartier France
  • National costume museum
  • Abissa festival of the N'Zima people
Discover Grand-Bassam
2 h from Abidjan

Assinie

A spit of sand between the Aby lagoon and the Atlantic. Still water, pirogues and boat trips on one side; ocean swell on the other. It's the weekend destination, with the best lodges in the country and easy access to the Ehotilé islands.

  • Lagoon and ocean facing each other
  • Watersports and pirogue trips
  • Ehotilé islands national park nearby
Discover Assinie
4 h west

Sassandra

A former trading post turned fishing port, sitting at the mouth of the river of the same name. Sheltered coves on either side, cliffs, and a fishing rhythm that sets the pace of the day. It's the quietest option, and the one travellers talk about most on the way home.

  • Sheltered coves
  • A working fishing port
  • Colonial remains above the river
Discover Sassandra
6 h west

San Pedro

The country's second port, and the gateway to the wildest beaches on the western coast. Less visited, rougher, with the forest coming down to the sand in places. Best combined with Taï National Park, a few hours inland.

  • Long, quiet beaches
  • Gateway to Taï National Park
  • Reachable by domestic flight
Discover San Pedro
1 h 30 from Abidjan

Jacqueville

An island joined to the mainland by a bridge, between the Ébrié lagoon and the ocean. Fishing villages, coconut groves, and a strip of sand that runs for kilometres. Close enough for a day trip, quiet enough to stay the night.

  • Doable as a day trip
  • Coconut groves and fishing villages
Discover Jacqueville
3 h from Abidjan

Grand-Lahou

The old town is slowly being reclaimed by the sea — a striking site, where you walk between colonial buildings the ocean is eating away. Next to it, the mouth of the Bandama river and Azagny National Park offer lagoons and mangroves.

  • A town being reclaimed by the ocean
  • Mangroves of Azagny National Park
Discover Grand-Lahou
