Adventure & ecotourism

Two World Heritage parks, one of the last primary rainforests in West Africa, and a mountain range in the west.

Adventure & ecotourism

From primary forest
to eighteen mountains.

The country holds three environments within a short distance: the rainforest of the south-west, the savanna of the north-east, and the western highlands around Man. We work in small groups, with local guides and community accommodation — which is what supports the surrounding villages, and therefore what protects the parks.

Where we take
our travellers

South-west

Taï National Park

One of the last stands of primary rainforest in West Africa, on the UNESCO World Heritage list. You can see chimpanzees, known for their tool use, along with many forest primates and birds. Groups stay small and a guide is mandatory.

  • UNESCO-listed primary forest
  • Chimpanzees and forest primates
  • Guided walks in small groups
Discover Taï National Park
North-east

Comoé National Park

One of the largest protected areas in West Africa, also UNESCO-listed. Savanna, gallery forest along the Comoé river, and savanna wildlife: antelope, warthog, hippopotamus, and remarkable birdlife.

  • Savanna and gallery forest
  • Savanna wildlife and birdlife
  • The country's largest park
Discover Comoé National Park
West

The Man region

The city of eighteen mountains. Waterfalls, liana bridges woven above the rivers, Mount Tonkoui and its viewpoints, the Dent de Man for walkers. The most dramatic landscape in the country.

  • Waterfalls and liana bridges
  • Mount Tonkoui
  • Mountain hiking
Discover The Man region
Inside Abidjan

Banco National Park

Thousands of hectares of rainforest inside the city itself. An arboretum, marked trails, and the famous fanico — the open-air traditional laundry on the riverbank. Ideal for half a day without leaving Abidjan.

  • Primary forest inside the city
  • Marked trails and an arboretum
  • Doable in half a day
Discover Banco National Park
Near Assinie

Ehotilé Islands

An archipelago in the Aby lagoon, protected as a national park. Mangroves, water birds, and islets the local communities consider sacred. Reached by pirogue from Assinie.

  • Mangroves and water birds
  • Reached by pirogue
  • Sacred islets
Discover Ehotilé Islands
Western border

Mount Nimba

A strict nature reserve on the World Heritage list, shared with Guinea and Liberia. A massif of exceptional biodiversity, with endemic species. Access is regulated and takes a long time to arrange.

  • UNESCO-listed reserve
  • Endemic species
  • Regulated access, arrange early
Discover Mount Nimba

Frequently asked

No, and it's better to know that before you come. Côte d'Ivoire is a country of forest and moist savanna: you see primates, birds, antelope, sometimes hippos and forest elephants. It is not the big five of the Kenyan plains. People who come for that leave disappointed; people who come for the forest leave converted.

Forest walks are flat but long, hot and humid. The Man region asks a little more because of the terrain. We match the days to your pace — just tell us what you're after.

For Taï and Mount Nimba, well ahead: access is controlled and permits take time. Allow at least two months. Banco and the Ehotilé islands can be arranged in a few days.

The dry season, November to April, for the tracks and for walking comfort. In the rainy season the forest is greener but access becomes difficult.
