Cultural trips

Around sixty languages, mask traditions that are still alive, recognised crafts and festivals that bring the whole country together.

Cultural trips

Living traditions,
not a museum.

Côte d'Ivoire has more than sixty cultural groups, and it shows in everything: the music, the cloth, the masks, the food. Our cultural trips are built around real encounters — craft workshops, markets, ceremonies when the village calendar allows — with Ivorian guides who explain the context and the etiquette.

Where we take
our travellers

3 h from Abidjan

Yamoussoukro

The political capital, grown out of Félix Houphouët-Boigny's home village. You visit the Basilica of Our Lady of Peace, consecrated in 1990 by John Paul II and one of the largest churches in the world, then the presidential palace and its crocodile lake.

  • Basilica of Our Lady of Peace
  • Presidential palace and its lake
  • Houphouët-Boigny Foundation
Discover Yamoussoukro
Northern Côte d'Ivoire

Korhogo and Senufo country

The capital of the north and the heart of Senufo culture. The nearby village of Fakaha produces the Korhogo cloth — cotton painted with plant pigments, now an emblem of the country. Around it, villages of carvers, blacksmiths and weavers.

  • Painted cloth from Fakaha
  • Carving and blacksmith villages
  • Senufo markets
Discover Korhogo and Senufo country
8 h from Abidjan

Man and the masks of the West

The Man region is Dan and Wè mask country, where mask appearances remain tied to village ceremonies rather than performance. You come with a local guide and an introduction: these are living traditions, not an attraction.

  • Dan and Wè traditions
  • Stilt dancing
  • Visits arranged with the families
Discover Man and the masks of the West
Your starting point

Abidjan

The Plateau and its towers, Saint Paul's Cathedral, the museum of civilisations, the Cocody artisan village, and the markets of Treichville and Adjamé. A well-run day in the city teaches you as much as a week elsewhere.

  • Museum of civilisations
  • Cocody artisan village
  • Treichville and Adjamé markets
Discover Abidjan
Central Côte d'Ivoire

Bouaké

The second city and the Baoulé capital, known for its carnival and its large market. Nearby, Katiola and its Mangoro potters, whose skill has passed from woman to woman for generations.

  • Bouaké carnival
  • Katiola pottery
  • The great market
Discover Bouaké
2 h from Abidjan

Tiégba

A village built on stilts above the lagoon, reached by pirogue. Life is organised around the water: fishing, movement, trade. Half a day that changes your perspective on the country.

  • Stilt village on the lagoon
  • Reached by pirogue
Discover Tiégba
